Paclingo turns typing into studying. Instead of just reading, you type — and what goes through your fingers sticks. The app's interface is in Brazilian Portuguese: it is made for Portuguese speakers learning English and programming.

🗓️ DAILY PRACTICE
• A short session ready for you: the reviews you are about to forget, new words and phrases from your current lesson.
• Day streaks, a daily goal and an optional reminder at the time you choose.

🗽 ENGLISH, FROM ZERO TO ADVANCED (A1 → C1)
• More than 5,000 real sentences with the Portuguese translation on top: you type each one several times, with less help each time.
• Answer your way: ⌨️ typing, 🎤 speaking or 🧩 building it with word blocks.
• Characters that say each sentence with lip sync, native-speaker recordings and a slow voice.
• New words introduced before each lesson, and "Minhas palavras" (My words) to keep your vocabulary.
• Stubborn words: the ones you miss most keep coming back until they stick.
• Spaced repetition brings back what you are about to forget.
• Dictation, shadowing, tap-a-word reading, and your own texts turned into lessons.
• Anki-compatible cards (export and import) and your own audio on your sentences.

🎧 LISTEN
• Play the course in order or build your own setlists: one sentence, part of a lesson, a whole lesson, a unit or everything.
• A mini-player that follows you around the app, with adjustable speed, repeats and pauses.
• On phones, audio plays while the app is open.

💬 AI CONVERSATION
• Practice English in everyday conversations and scenarios, with your writing corrected and explanations in Portuguese.
• AI is optional and is only used after you allow it.

💻 DART & FLUTTER AND C# & .NET
• Type real code, step by step, and see the result.
• Short theory in every lesson, quizzes and projects, from beginner to advanced.

🎮 TYPING ARCADE
• Racing, rally, word rain and more. In English, the games use the words from your lessons and say each one out loud.
• Growing levels, combos, personal bests and a player ranking.

☁️ YOUR PROGRESS IN THE CLOUD
• Sign in with email and password and pick up where you left off, on any device.
• No ads. You can delete your account and all your data inside the app.

The English course sentences and recordings come from the Tatoeba project (CC BY license).
